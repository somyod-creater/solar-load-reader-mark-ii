Imported Net Load: ใช้ Import (kWh) = โหลดคงเหลือปัจจุบัน (หลังหักโซลาร์ Phase 1 ที่ติดอยู่แล้ว) เป็นฐาน แล้วหักด้วย Designed Solar Phase 2 ที่กำลังจะติดตั้ง → Net Load = Import − Phase 2 คือโหลดที่ยังต้องซื้อจากกริดหลังติด Phase 2 (เส้น Consumption = โหลดเดิมก่อนมีโซลาร์, Solar Phase 1 = Inverter Yield ปัจจุบัน)
💡 Solar Only (No Battery): The orange line represents the net power still purchased from the grid. The key issue is the red area at noon, which represents surplus solar generation that is curtailed/wasted due to lack of storage. This illustrates why a battery system is beneficial.
💡 With BESS Battery: The pink area shows the peak demand shaved by the battery (Peak Shaving). The battery stores surplus midday solar energy and discharges it during peak periods, flattening the net grid demand profile to reduce both energy charges and Peak Demand charges (Demand Charge).
💡 Battery Operation: The green area (Positive) represents the battery charging power (typically during midday when there is surplus solar). The pink area (Negative) represents the battery discharging power (evening, night, or peak hours). The purple line (SOC) shows the battery's State of Charge (%).
💡 Comparison Summary: Net grid power purchased comparing Solar Only vs. Solar + Battery. A lower and flatter green curve indicates greater cost savings and more effective peak demand reduction.
